The tag has no RTC, so it starts at 00:00 after every power cut and needs a fresh TIME(). The face itself is kept in flash and survives.

Expect the link to drop a second or two after each push: the panel refresh blocks the tag for ~2 s, longer than Bluetooth will wait for it. The template is already stored by then, and the next action reconnects on its own.
