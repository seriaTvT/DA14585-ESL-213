The tag has no RTC, so it starts at 00:00 after every power cut and needs a fresh TIME(). The face itself is kept in flash and survives.

The panel takes ~2 s to refresh after a push, and the tag stays connected throughout. If the link does drop, the next action reconnects on its own.

The panel is one bit per pixel, so tone is faked with dithering — try Atkinson for portraits and line art, Floyd–Steinberg for detailed photos. Threshold only matters when the dither is Ordered or Threshold.

An image replaces the clock until you push a template again, and it is not kept in flash — a power cut brings back the stored face.

Command reference
CLEAR(c)Fill the screen. 0 black, 1 white.
POINT(x,y,color=)Single pixel. A fat one is LINE(x,y,x,y,width=n).
LINE(x1,y1,x2,y2,color= width=) width is the stroke, default 1.
RECT(x1,y1,x2,y2,color= width= fill=) fill=1 for solid.
CIRCLE(x,y,r,color= width= fill=)
TEXT(x,y,'text',color= bg= scale= align= font=) One fixed-pitch 5×7 font so far. Glyph cell is 6×7 px times scale. bg defaults to 1, so text is opaque — use color=1 with bg=0 for white on black. align= moves the anchor: 0 left, 1 centre, 2 right. font=1 is the 16×24 digits (digits and : only; anything else is blank).
ROTATE(deg)Degrees only — 0, 90, 180, 270. 90 and 270 are landscape (250×122). ROTATE(3) used to mean 270 and is now refused, because the two spellings collided exactly where it mattered.
INVERT(x,y,w,h) Flip every pixel in a box — width and height, not a second corner. Put it last: text drawn afterwards paints its whole glyph cell and would blank the box.
EVERY(n) Minutes between repaints, 1–1440, default 1. Boundaries are absolute, so EVERY(60) lands on the hour. Stored with the face; a full refresh is the most expensive thing the tag does.
TIME(s)Seconds since 2000-01-01. Applied on arrival, never stored — sent for you by Sync time.
RESET()Begin a new template. Sent for you by Push, so repeat pushes replace rather than stack.
{H} {N} {S}hour (0–23), minute, second
{h} {P}hour on a 12-hour clock (1–12), and AM/PM
{y} {m} {d}year, month, day
{M}month name (JAN…DEC)
{w} {W}weekday number, weekday name (SUN…SAT)
{j} {J}day of the year (1–366), days in this year (365 or 366)
{D}days in this month, 28–31 — the length {d} runs against, as {J} is for {j}
{V} {G}ISO 8601 week number and its week-year. Early January can belong to week 52/53 of the previous year, which is why the year is separate from {y}.
{u}raw seconds since the 2000 epoch
{VER}firmware tag — HEMA1
{H:02d}width/zero-pad suffix, printf-style

Variables work in numeric arguments too, not just in text, and arguments are expressions: + - * / %, parentheses and unary minus, all integer. That is what lets a face draw itself rather than just label itself — RECT(4,70,4+{d}*8,82,fill=1) is a bar that grows across the month. Division by zero gives 0, and anything malformed gives 0 rather than failing, matching the firmware.

Values re-expand on every minute tick, which is what makes the tag a clock rather than a picture frame. Anything not listed here is ignored by the firmware — the preview flags it rather than failing silently.
